Diagnosis 01

Leak or burst pipe

Urgency Act now

What it might be

  • A cracked or burst supply pipe
  • A failed fitting, valve or connector
  • A leaking water heater or appliance hose

Do this now

  1. Turn off your main water shut-off valve.
  2. Keep away from water near outlets or appliances. Only switch power off at the breaker if you can reach it from a dry spot.
  3. Move valuables and take photos for your records.

Diagnosis 02

Clogged drain

Urgency Soon

What it might be

  • Grease, hair or soap build-up
  • Something that shouldn't have gone down the drain
  • A blocked main line, if several drains are slow at once

Do this now

  1. Stop running water into the blocked drain.
  2. Avoid chemical drain cleaners; they can damage pipes and cause burns.
  3. If several drains are slow or gurgling, stop using water and call: it may be the main line.

Diagnosis 03

No hot water

Urgency Soon

What it might be

  • A tripped breaker or failed element (electric heaters)
  • A pilot or ignition problem (gas heaters)
  • A faulty thermostat or sediment build-up

Do this now

  1. Check whether every tap is affected, or just one.
  2. Electric heater: check its breaker. Gas heater: only follow the label's instructions.
  3. If the tank is leaking, close the cold-water valve on top of the heater.

Diagnosis 04

Low water pressure

Urgency Routine

What it might be

  • A clogged aerator or shower head
  • A partly closed valve or failing pressure regulator
  • A hidden leak, or work on the city supply

Do this now

  1. Check whether it's one fixture or the whole house.
  2. Unscrew and rinse the tap aerator or shower head.
  3. Make sure the main shut-off valve is fully open, and ask a neighbour if theirs is low too.

Diagnosis 05

Sewer smell or backup

Urgency Act now

What it might be

  • A blocked or damaged main sewer line
  • Tree roots in the line
  • An overloaded system after heavy rain

Do this now

  1. Stop using water. Every flush or shower adds to the backup.
  2. Keep people and pets away; sewage is a health hazard.
  3. Don't use chemical drain cleaners. Open windows for fresh air.

Safety first

Leave the building now.

  1. Get everyone out, including pets. Leave the door open behind you.
  2. Don't switch lights or appliances on or off, light anything, or use a phone while you're inside.
  3. Once you're well away from the building, call 911 or your gas company's emergency line.
  4. Don't go back inside until the gas company or fire department says it's safe.

A gas smell is an emergency for your gas company or 911 — not a plumbing enquiry. This site doesn't take gas-leak requests.
